**This is an alpha.** It works and saves your songs, but expect rough edges. Project files may still change between versions; files from this version will keep opening in later ones. Please report problems in [Issues](https://github.com/jerichosiahaya/ritmo/issues).

Ritmo is a DAW for Linux that runs on just a laptop, with sample chopping built in.

## Install

- **AppImage** (x86_64; any distribution with glibc 2.35 or newer: Ubuntu 22.04+, Debian 12+, Fedora, Arch, …): download `Ritmo-0.2.0-alpha.1-x86_64.AppImage` below, then `chmod +x Ritmo-*.AppImage` and run it.
- **Arch Linux:** the `ritmo` package in the AUR.
- **From source:** see the [README](https://github.com/jerichosiahaya/ritmo#install).

Start with **File ▸ Open a demo**: a lo-fi loop, a boom-bap beat made by chopping a break, and a house track.

## What's in it

- **Patterns and a playlist.** A step sequencer for drums and a piano roll for melodies, as many patterns as you like. Place clips anywhere; move, copy, resize (a stretched clip repeats its pattern) and delete them. Songs up to 999 bars, a loop range, and playback from anywhere you click on the ruler.
- **Piano roll** with note length, velocity, box selection, copy/paste, duplicate, snap and arrow-key nudges.
- **Chop sampler.** Load WAV, FLAC, MP3, OGG or AIFF and chop it onto 16 pads at its hits, in even slices or by tapping along. Pitch, reverse and level per pad; chops are notes like any other.
- **Instruments:** synth drums, an FM electric piano, a bass, and a new **Synth** (saw to square, detune, resonant filter with its own envelope). Every channel has pitch, tone, attack and release.
- **Mixer** with a 3-band EQ and a filter on every track, a shared tempo-synced delay and reverb, peak meters, and a lo-fi master chain (tone, crush, wobble, vinyl, reverb).
- **Chord stamps** from the browser, a metronome, and a typing-keyboard piano with octave shift.
- **Projects** saved as readable files with autosave, undo and redo for every edit, and WAV export.
- **Audio settings:** output device and buffer size.

## Not yet

- **No plugins.** CLAP instruments and effects come next (v0.3); LV2 and VST3 later. Windows plugins will be usable through yabridge once VST3 or CLAP hosting is in.
- No audio recording or audio clips on the playlist, and no MIDI controller input yet.
- ALSA output only (PipeWire and PulseAudio serve it); no JACK yet.
- The AppImage is x86_64 only.

## Help wanted

Ritmo was tested in containers on Ubuntu 22.04 and 24.04, Arch and Fedora 45, and its engine is covered by tests, but it needs time on real computers and sound cards. If the sound crackles, try a bigger buffer in **⚙ Audio**, and tell us your distribution, sound card and the buffer that works.
